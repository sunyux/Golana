Golana · browser build

SPHERE, a Biosphere view for the FM-1

Golana (a microKORG-style synth firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1) running in your browser, with a fourth HOME drawing: the notes you play land as stars on a pitch wheel, joined in the order played, the newest a ringed planet, over a turning galaxy.

Open the simulated FM-1 →
  1. Click Switch on.
  2. Click GLO until the tab reads GLO · SETUP, then scroll on KNOB 3 until HOME shows SPHERE.
  3. Click HOME and play: computer keys Z … ' and Q … 0.
